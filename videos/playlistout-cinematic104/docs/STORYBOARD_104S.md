## Cinematic camera and transfer

- 13 chapters are **narrative stations on one connected world**. The camera starts trucking 1.25s before each 8s boundary and finishes 1.25s after it; no old-style 0.65s wipe.
- From selected 2s intervals inside station 3 onward, the camera pushes in toward the operating interface, the earlier left title retires, then the lens pulls back to expose the next connected space. This creates actual wide / medium / close visual coverage.
- A playlist passport follows the global journey, changes chapter labels, and physically crosses the foreground with four inertial motion variants. The die-cut paper path and bottom journal thread connect through every seam.
- Every individual large panel retains readable type for at least ~2s in its centered closeup; the ticket covers it only during intentional scene transfers.
- On-screen chapter counters, duration badges and BPM labels have been removed for a more filmic presentation.
