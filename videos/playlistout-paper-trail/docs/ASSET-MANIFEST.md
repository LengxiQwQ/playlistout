## Code-generated visuals

All other imagery is drawn in the composition:

- notebook page, ruling, red margin, binder holes, tape, paper shadows
- six platform tickets
- abstract night-sky cover art
- XLSX, JSON, CSV, TXT, M3U8 and Markdown file surfaces
- multi-sheet workbook, ZIP folder, envelope, terminal chrome
- player cards, monogram logos, doodles and the note mascot
- end-cover bookmark, ribbons and paper fibers

No stock footage, external illustrations or downloaded UI screenshots are used
as rendered pixels.

## Music & sound

See `docs/AUDIO.md`. Everything is synthesized:
- no music library samples
- no recordings
- no sample packs
- no third-party sound effects

The score and effects are original works released under CC BY 4.0 for this
promotional film. The generator is included and deterministic (seeded).
