# Audio Licensing

All audio in this folder is original, procedurally synthesised material created
for the Playlist Out promotional film by `tools/make_score.py` and
`tools/make_intro_prelude.py`.

- `soundtrack-v3.wav` — final 58 s track: a 2 s paper/pencil cold-open prelude
  followed by the original 56 s score.
- `soundtrack.wav` — the 56 s score used by v1/v2.
- `music-only.wav` — music stem for the 56 s score.
- `sfx-only.wav` — foley / UI effects stem for the 56 s score.

There are no third-party samples, loops, recordings, commercial songs, or
lyrics. The composition, prelude and sound design are generated from code.

The audio is released under **CC0 1.0 Universal (public-domain dedication)**
and may alternatively be used under the **MIT License** together with the rest
of this project. It is suitable for publication on Bilibili, YouTube, GitHub,
and the Playlist Out website.

The score is an original work made for this film; no external music licence is
required for distribution of the film.
